# Code of conduct

Dualyrics follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The short version is below; the full text at that link applies.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Dualyrics to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. People come here from many countries and write in many languages; patience with imperfect English (or Persian) is part of that.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use Dualyrics.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and wherever someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, [morpheusadam](https://github.com/morpheusadam), through a private channel: GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue, or a private security advisory in this repository if nothing else fits. Reports are handled in confidence. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may block contributors temporarily or permanently, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
